### 🔨 `$ ls ./currently-building`

- **Text-to-SQL at scale.** Natural-language querying over Tamil Nadu's cadastral (land records) database of **1.27 TB and 890 tables**, using a self-hosted **Qwen** model; being launched on the Tamil Nadu GIS website. Extended to the crop survey (~44M rows) and State Scholarship Portal databases.
- **MCP server over Government Orders.** Deployed on TNEGA's internal server so officers can search **217 Tamil Nadu Government Orders**; demoed to IAS officers in hands-on sessions.
- **RecruitAI.** An LLM-powered hiring screener for Digital India Corporation that turns job descriptions into structured eligibility criteria and gives each candidate an evidence-grounded decision.
- **[MindMatcher](https://github.com/parharti/MindMaster).** A C++17 inference engine built from scratch that parses ONNX models and executes their graphs. Now working on CNN support and faster matmul (tiling, AVX2 SIMD, multithreading). *Work in progress.*
